# Code of conduct

Open Tracker follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and respectful, and welcome people of every background and experience level. Focus on what helps people stay safe from floods. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not accepted. Report problems to the maintainers privately.
